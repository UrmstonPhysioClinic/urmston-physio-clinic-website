The People Behind Your Care

Our Physiotherapists

Four experienced, HCPC-registered physiotherapists, each with distinct specialist expertise, all committed to the same standard of patient-centred care.

Carl Healy

Clinic Director
BSc Physiotherapy, Salford 2015
HCPC registration PH106684

Clinical Background

Carl is Clinic Director at Urmston Physio Clinic, managing the day-to-day running of the practice alongside his clinical caseload. He graduated from the University of Salford in 2015 and has since developed a strong reputation for thorough, effective musculoskeletal physiotherapy.

Areas of Expertise

His clinical work focuses on musculoskeletal and neuromuscular physiotherapy, with particular interest in the shoulder, hip, sacroiliac joint and lower back. He takes a movement-focused, evidence-informed approach, aiming to find the underlying drivers of a problem rather than simply addressing the surface symptoms.

Clinical Philosophy

Carl's primary aim is always to identify the underlying drivers of dysfunction, not just manage symptoms, and to restore optimal function that lasts. He works collaboratively with patients, ensuring they understand their condition and are active participants in their recovery.

Outside the Clinic

Carl is a keen runner and cyclist who regularly hikes in the Lake District. His background also includes white-water kayaking, ballet and extensive martial arts training, all of which inform his understanding of movement and physical performance.

Roxanna Healy

Senior Physiotherapist
Newcastle Polytechnic, 1987
HCPC registration PH37932

Clinical Background

Roxanna qualified from Newcastle Polytechnic in 1987, giving her nearly four decades of clinical experience. Her career spans two distinct disciplines: she began specialising in paediatric neurological and MSK physiotherapy, building deep expertise in complex neurological rehabilitation, before moving into musculoskeletal physiotherapy where she has worked as a clinical specialist for many years.

Areas of Expertise

She has developed extensive expertise in complex neurological rehabilitation and combines this with hands-on MSK practice to deliver care that is truly holistic and highly effective.

Clinical Philosophy

Roxanna brings a holistic, functional approach to rehabilitation, drawing on her neurological background to support patients with diverse and often complex presentations.

Outside the Clinic

At weekends Roxanna enjoys walks along the Formby coast, where she relaxes and watches the local red squirrel population amid the sand dunes and pine forest.

Diana Satalova

Biomechanics & Movement
BSc Physiotherapy, Brighton 2011
HCPC registration PH100077

Clinical Background

Since graduating from the University of Brighton in 2011, Diana has continuously developed her knowledge and clinical skills, driven by a genuine passion for understanding how the human body moves, compensates, and heals.

Areas of Expertise

Diana has built a clinical focus on body movement, biomechanics, neuro-kinetic therapy and breathing therapy. She takes a full-body approach to assessment and treatment, working on the basis that the source of pain is often found somewhere other than where the symptoms are felt. This perspective has proved particularly effective for patients who have struggled to find answers with other practitioners.

Clinical Philosophy

Diana is committed to ongoing learning and regularly attends courses and seminars to develop her clinical skills further. She finds great satisfaction in seeing the positive impact that a well-targeted, thorough assessment and treatment plan can have on people's lives.

Outside the Clinic

Outside the clinic she studies languages, goes hiking, and attends health education events, a reflection of her genuine love of learning and continuous development.

Steve Sharples

MSK & Orthopaedic Physiotherapist
BSc Sports Science, 1987 BSc Physiotherapy, 1992
HCPC registration PH44642

Clinical Background

Steve brings over 30 years of clinical experience to Urmston Physio Clinic. He completed a BSc in Sports Science in 1987 before qualifying as a physiotherapist in 1992, a dual academic background that gives him a particularly strong grounding in movement science and physical performance alongside his clinical training.

Areas of Expertise

Since qualifying he has worked across NHS and private settings, developing broad and deep expertise in musculoskeletal and orthopaedic physiotherapy. His clinical experience spans A&E, hydrotherapy, rheumatology and shoulder clinics, and he has held senior roles including Extended Scope Practitioner in Musculoskeletal and Rheumatology and Orthopaedic Physiotherapy Specialist.

Clinical Philosophy

Steve is known for a measured, thorough, holistic approach to assessment and a genuine commitment to good patient outcomes.

Outside the Clinic

Outside the clinic, Steve is a keen runner with an extensive background in mountaineering and hiking. He also has a long history in martial arts, including Judo and Jujitsu.

All our physiotherapists are registered with the Health and Care Professions Council (HCPC) and are members of the Chartered Society of Physiotherapy (CSP). The CSP is the professional, educational and trade union body for the UK's chartered physiotherapists, physiotherapy students and support workers. You can check any physiotherapist's registration on the HCPC register.
