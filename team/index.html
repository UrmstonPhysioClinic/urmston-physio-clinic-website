The People Behind Your Care

Our Team

Four experienced, HCPC-registered physiotherapists, each with distinct specialist expertise, all committed to the same standard of patient-centred care.

Clinic Director

Carl Healy

MSK Physiotherapist
HCPC Registered CSP Member BSc Physiotherapy, Salford 2015

Carl is Clinic Director at Urmston Physio Clinic, managing the day-to-day running of the practice alongside his clinical caseload. He graduated from the University of Salford in 2015 and has since developed a strong reputation for thorough, effective musculoskeletal physiotherapy.

His clinical work focuses on musculoskeletal and neuromuscular physiotherapy, with particular interest in shoulder and pelvic dysfunction. He takes a movement-focused, evidence-informed approach, aiming to find the underlying drivers of a problem rather than simply addressing the surface symptoms.

Outside the clinic, Carl is a keen runner and cyclist who regularly hikes in the Lake District. His background also includes white-water kayaking, ballet and extensive martial arts training, all of which inform his understanding of movement and physical performance.

Senior Physiotherapist

Roxanna Healy

MSK Physiotherapist
HCPC Registered CSP Member Newcastle Polytechnic, 1987

Roxanna qualified from Newcastle Polytechnic in 1987, giving her nearly four decades of clinical experience. Her career spans two distinct disciplines: she began specialising in paediatric neurological physiotherapy, building deep expertise in complex neurological rehabilitation, before moving into musculoskeletal physiotherapy where she has worked as a clinical specialist for many years.

She brings a holistic, functional approach to rehabilitation, drawing on her neurological background to support patients with diverse and often complex presentations. Her breadth of experience is something few practitioners can match.

At weekends Roxanna enjoys walks along the Formby coast, where she relaxes and watches the local red squirrel population.

Physiotherapist

Diana Satalova

Biomechanics & Movement
HCPC Registered CSP Member BSc Physiotherapy, Brighton 2011

Since graduating from the University of Brighton in 2011, Diana has built a clinical focus on body movement, biomechanics, neuro-kinetic therapy and breathing therapy. She takes a full-body approach to assessment and treatment, working on the basis that the source of pain is often found somewhere other than where the symptoms are felt.

This perspective has proved particularly effective for patients who have struggled to find answers with other practitioners. Diana is committed to ongoing learning and regularly attends courses and seminars to develop her clinical skills further.

Outside the clinic she studies languages, goes hiking, and attends health education events.

Physiotherapist

Steve Sharples

MSK & Orthopaedic Physiotherapist
HCPC Registered CSP Member BSc Sports Science, 1987 BSc Physiotherapy, 1992

Steve brings over 30 years of clinical experience to Urmston Physio Clinic. He completed a BSc in Sports Science in 1987 before going on to qualify as a physiotherapist in 1992, a dual academic background that gives him a particularly strong grounding in movement science and physical performance alongside his clinical training.

Since qualifying he has worked across NHS and private settings, developing broad and deep expertise in musculoskeletal and orthopaedic physiotherapy. His clinical experience spans A&E, hydrotherapy, rheumatology and shoulder clinics, and he has held senior roles including Extended Scope Practitioner in Musculoskeletal and Rheumatology and Orthopaedic Physiotherapy Specialist.

Steve is known for a measured, thorough approach to assessment and a genuine commitment to good patient outcomes. His personal interests and hobbies will be added here shortly.
