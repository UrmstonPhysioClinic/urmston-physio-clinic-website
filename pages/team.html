Carl Healy

Clinic Director & MSK Physiotherapist
HCPC Registered CSP Member BSc Physiotherapy, Salford 2015

Clinical Background

Carl is Clinic Director at Urmston Physio Clinic, overseeing the day-to-day running and long-term development of the practice alongside his active clinical caseload. He graduated from the University of Salford in 2015 and has since built a strong reputation as a thorough, thoughtful, and effective musculoskeletal physiotherapist.

Areas of Expertise

Carl specialises in musculoskeletal and neuromuscular physiotherapy, with a strong focus on movement assessment, dysfunction analysis, and long-term physical wellbeing. He has a particular clinical interest in shoulder and pelvic dysfunction — areas where precise assessment and movement-based rehabilitation can make a profound and lasting difference.

Clinical Philosophy

Carl adopts a movement-focused, evidence-informed approach. His primary aim is to identify the underlying drivers of dysfunction — not simply manage symptoms — and to restore optimal function that lasts. He works collaboratively with patients, ensuring they understand their condition and are active participants in their recovery.

Outside the Clinic

Carl is a keen runner and cyclist who regularly spends time hiking in the Lake District fells. His background includes white-water kayaking, ballet, and extensive martial arts training — experiences that have given him a uniquely broad understanding of movement, control, and physical performance.

Roxanna Healy

Senior MSK Physiotherapist
HCPC Registered CSP Member Newcastle Polytechnic, 1987

Clinical Background

Roxanna trained at Newcastle Polytechnic, qualifying in 1987 — bringing over three decades of clinical experience to the team. Her career spans two major physiotherapy disciplines, giving her an exceptionally broad and deep clinical foundation that few practitioners can match.

Areas of Expertise

Following qualification, Roxanna specialised in paediatric neurological physiotherapy, developing extensive expertise in complex neurological rehabilitation. She later transitioned into musculoskeletal physiotherapy, where she has worked as a clinical specialist for many years — combining her neurological background with hands-on MSK practice to deliver genuinely holistic and highly effective care.

Clinical Philosophy

Roxanna has a strong interest in holistic, functional rehabilitation. She draws on her wide-ranging experience across both neurological and musculoskeletal practice to support patients with diverse and often complex presentations — bringing patience, depth of knowledge, and genuine clinical curiosity to every consultation.

Outside the Clinic

Roxanna enjoys weekend visits to Formby coast, where she relaxes among the sand dunes and pine forest and enjoys watching the local red squirrel population.

Diana Satalova

Physiotherapist — Biomechanics & Movement
HCPC Registered CSP Member BSc Physiotherapy, Brighton 2011

Clinical Background

Diana graduated from the University of Brighton in 2011 with a BSc in Physiotherapy. Since qualifying, she has continuously developed her knowledge and clinical skills, driven by a genuine passion for understanding how the body moves, compensates, and heals.

Areas of Expertise

Diana has developed a distinctive clinical focus on body movement, biomechanics, neuro-kinetic therapy, and breathing therapy. She takes a holistic, full-body approach — recognising, through years of clinical experience, that the source of a patient's pain is often located somewhere different from where the symptoms are felt. This systems-thinking approach delivers results for patients who have struggled to find answers elsewhere.

Clinical Philosophy

Diana is passionate about helping patients improve their movement, function, and overall wellbeing. She is dedicated to ongoing professional development, regularly attending courses and seminars to expand her knowledge and clinical repertoire.

Outside the Clinic

An enthusiastic language student, Diana enjoys hiking and attending health education seminars — a reflection of her genuine love of learning and continuous development.
