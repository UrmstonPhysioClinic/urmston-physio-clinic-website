Back & Neck Pain

Back and neck pain is one of the most common — and most treatable — conditions we see. Whether you're dealing with a sudden injury, postural strain from desk work, a disc problem, or long-standing chronic pain, our physiotherapists take the time to assess fully and identify the true cause.

Treatment combines manual therapy, targeted exercise, movement re-education, and lifestyle guidance to resolve pain and prevent recurrence.

Disc herniationPostural strainFacet joint painSciaticaCervicogenic headachesWhiplash
02

Sports Injury Rehabilitation

Getting injured is frustrating — but recovering well is what matters most. Our physiotherapists understand sport, athletic movement, and what it takes to return to full performance safely.

We go beyond pain relief to address the underlying biomechanical factors that contributed to your injury. From acute muscle tears to chronic overuse injuries, we create a structured return-to-sport programme tailored to your sport and level.

Muscle strains and tearsLigament sprainsOveruse injuriesRunner's injuriesAchilles tendinopathyReturn to sport
03

Shoulder & Upper Limb

The shoulder is one of the most complex joints in the body and prone to a wide range of painful conditions. Carl Healy has a particular specialist interest in shoulder dysfunction, and the team have extensive combined experience across all upper limb problems.

We assess not just the joint in isolation, but how it interacts with the neck, thorax, and surrounding musculature — finding the true source of dysfunction and restoring full, pain-free movement.

Rotator cuff tearsFrozen shoulderShoulder impingementTennis and golfer's elbowWrist and hand painAC joint injuries
04

Hip, Knee & Lower Limb

Lower limb pain can significantly impact your ability to walk, exercise, and live fully. We assess the hip, knee, ankle, and foot as an interconnected system — understanding how dysfunction in one area affects the others.

From arthritis and ligament injuries to repetitive strain conditions, we deliver hands-on treatment and progressive rehabilitation to restore strength, stability, and confidence.

Hip pain and arthritisKnee ligament injuriesPatellofemoral painIT band syndromePlantar fasciitisAnkle sprains
05

Post-Operative Rehabilitation

Surgery is the beginning of recovery, not the end. A well-structured rehabilitation programme is essential to achieving the best possible outcome from any orthopaedic procedure.

Our physiotherapists have extensive experience guiding patients through recovery from a wide range of surgeries, progressing your rehabilitation at the right pace — safe, thorough, and built around your goals.

Hip replacementKnee replacementACL reconstructionRotator cuff repairSpinal surgeryFoot and ankle surgery
06

Movement & Biomechanics

Not all physiotherapy is about injury. Our movement and biomechanics service is designed for those who want to move more efficiently, reduce injury risk, or optimise physical performance — whether you are an athlete, gym-goer, or simply someone who wants to stay active and pain-free.

Drawing on Diana Satalova's expertise in neuro-kinetic therapy, biomechanics, and breathing therapy, we provide detailed movement analysis and targeted intervention to address patterns that limit you.
