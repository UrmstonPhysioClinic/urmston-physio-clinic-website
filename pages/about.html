Who We Are

Physiotherapy That Treats the Whole Person

Urmston Physio Clinic is a private musculoskeletal physiotherapy clinic based in Urmston, Manchester. We are a team of four HCPC-registered physiotherapists, bringing together decades of specialist expertise across musculoskeletal, neurological, biomechanical, and sports physiotherapy.

Our clinic is built on a simple but powerful belief: that the best physiotherapy goes beyond managing symptoms. It identifies the true cause of a problem, addresses it systematically, and equips patients with the knowledge and capacity to stay well for the long term.

Every member of our team has developed distinct specialist interests alongside their broad MSK expertise — from shoulder and pelvic dysfunction to neurological rehabilitation to biomechanics and neuro-kinetic therapy. Whatever has brought you to us, you will be seen by someone with the depth of knowledge to help.

Our Principles

How We Work

01

Find the Cause

We never treat symptoms in isolation. Our assessments are thorough by design — looking at the whole body, movement patterns, history, and lifestyle to understand what is truly driving your pain or dysfunction.

02

Evidence-Informed Practice

Everything we do is grounded in current clinical research. Our physiotherapists are committed to ongoing professional development and bring the latest evidence to their clinical decision-making.

03

Tailored to You

No two patients are the same. Your treatment plan is built around your specific condition, goals, lifestyle, and timeline. We work with you, not just on you.

04

Long-Term Thinking

We want you to recover fully and stay well. Education, self-management strategies, and preventive care are central to everything we do — not just short-term symptom relief.

05

Honest and Transparent

We give you clear, honest information about your condition, realistic expectations about recovery, and a straightforward assessment of how we can help. You will never be kept in the dark.

06

Patient Partnership

Recovery is a partnership. We provide the expertise, hands-on treatment, and guidance — and we help you become an informed, engaged participant in your own recovery.

Professional Standards

Registered and Accredited

All physiotherapists at Urmston Physio Clinic are registered with the Health and Care Professions Council (HCPC) and are members of the Chartered Society of Physiotherapy (CSP) — the UK's statutory and professional standards bodies for physiotherapy.
