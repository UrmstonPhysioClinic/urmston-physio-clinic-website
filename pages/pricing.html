Insurance

Recognised by Major Insurers

If you hold a private medical insurance policy, your physiotherapy treatment at Urmston Physio Clinic may be covered. We are recognised providers for the following insurers:

BUPA Recognised
AXA Health Recognised
Aviva Recognised
WPA Recognised
How to use your insurance: Before booking, contact your insurer to confirm that physiotherapy is covered under your policy and to obtain an authorisation or claim number. You will typically need to provide this when you attend your first appointment. If you are unsure whether your insurer is on our recognised list or how to proceed, please call us on 0161 748 4100 and we will be happy to help.
