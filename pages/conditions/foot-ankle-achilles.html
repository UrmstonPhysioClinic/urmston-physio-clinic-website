Foot, Ankle & Achilles Pain

The foot and ankle form the foundation of every step you take — making pain in this region particularly disruptive. From plantar fasciitis first thing in the morning, to Achilles pain that stops you running, to ankle instability after a sprain, we see a wide range of foot and ankle conditions and have the expertise to assess and treat them effectively.

About This Condition

Understanding Foot, Ankle & Achilles Pain

Foot and ankle problems rarely exist in isolation. The way the foot functions affects the knee, hip, and lower back — and conversely, problems higher up the chain can change the way load is distributed through the foot. Our assessment examines the whole lower limb to understand the full picture.

For conditions like Achilles tendinopathy and plantar fasciitis, the evidence strongly supports a progressive loading approach — gradually increasing the demand on the tendon or plantar fascia to stimulate healing and build resilience. We are experienced in guiding patients through this process safely and effectively.

Signs & Symptoms

Common Symptoms

These are the most common presentations we see — though every patient is different, and your physiotherapist will assess your individual symptoms thoroughly.

Heel Pain

Sharp or aching pain under the heel, particularly on the first steps in the morning — the hallmark of plantar fasciitis.

Achilles Pain

Pain, stiffness, or thickening at the back of the heel or lower calf, often worse in the morning and after exercise.

Ankle Instability

A feeling that the ankle may give way, often following a previous sprain that has not fully healed.

Arch Pain

Aching in the arch of the foot, sometimes associated with flat feet or excessive pronation during walking or running.

Swelling and Bruising

Following an ankle sprain or other injury — indicating ligament or soft tissue damage requiring assessment.

Toe and Forefoot Pain

Pain in the ball of the foot, toes, or forefoot — including conditions such as Morton's neuroma or metatarsalgia.

Our Approach

How We Treat Foot, Ankle & Achilles Pain

Your treatment plan will be tailored specifically to you — but here is what a typical course of physiotherapy for foot, ankle & achilles pain looks like at Urmston Physio Clinic.

  • 01
    Full Lower Limb and Foot AssessmentEvaluation of the foot, ankle, and lower limb mechanics to understand the cause of your pain and any contributing factors from higher up the chain.
  • 02
    Manual TherapyJoint mobilisation of the ankle, subtalar, and foot joints, combined with soft tissue techniques to reduce pain and restore movement.
  • 03
    Progressive Loading ProgrammeEvidence-based rehabilitation for Achilles tendinopathy and plantar fasciitis, building tissue capacity safely and progressively.
  • 04
    Ankle Stability RehabilitationBalance and proprioceptive training combined with strength work to restore ankle stability and reduce the risk of re-injury.
  • 05
    Gait and Biomechanics AnalysisAssessing how you walk and run to identify any movement patterns that may be contributing to your foot or ankle problem.
