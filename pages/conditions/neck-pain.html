Condition

Neck Pain

Neck pain is increasingly common in a world of screens, desk work, and sedentary lifestyles — but it is rarely something you just have to put up with. Whether your neck pain came on suddenly after an injury or has been slowly building over months, our physiotherapists can assess what is causing it and provide targeted, effective treatment to get you moving freely again.

About This Condition

Understanding Neck Pain

Neck pain rarely has just one cause. It often involves a combination of joint stiffness, muscle tension, movement habits, and postural factors — and these need to be assessed and addressed together for treatment to be effective and lasting.

Our physiotherapists assess the full cervical spine, looking at joint mobility, muscle function, nerve sensitivity, and the relationship between the neck, upper back, and shoulders. This comprehensive picture allows us to target treatment precisely where it is needed.

Signs & Symptoms

Common Symptoms

These are the most common presentations we see — though every patient is different, and your physiotherapist will assess your individual symptoms thoroughly.

Neck Stiffness

Reduced ability to turn, tilt, or flex the head, often with a feeling of tightness or resistance.

Headaches

Pain originating from the neck that refers into the base of the skull, temples, or behind the eyes.

Arm Pain or Tingling

Pain, numbness, or tingling that radiates from the neck into the shoulder, arm, or fingers, often caused by nerve irritation.

Muscle Tightness

Persistent tension in the neck, upper back, and shoulder muscles, sometimes with palpable knots.

Pain on Movement

Sharp or aching pain triggered by turning the head, looking up, or holding certain positions.

Postural Discomfort

Aching in the neck and upper back after prolonged sitting at a desk or looking at a screen.

Our Approach

How We Treat Neck Pain

Your treatment plan will be tailored specifically to you — but here is what a typical course of physiotherapy for neck pain looks like at Urmston Physio Clinic.

  • 01
    Cervical AssessmentThorough evaluation of the neck joints, muscles, nerves, and posture to identify the specific cause of your pain.
  • 02
    Manual TherapyJoint mobilisation and manipulation, soft tissue release, and neural mobilisation techniques to restore movement and reduce pain.
  • 03
    Postural Analysis and CorrectionIdentifying the postural habits and muscle imbalances contributing to your neck pain, with practical strategies to address them.
  • 04
    Exercise ProgrammeTargeted strengthening and mobility exercises for the neck, upper back, and shoulder girdle to support and protect the cervical spine.
  • 05
    Ergonomic AdviceGuidance on workstation setup, screen use, and daily habits to reduce the strain on your neck and prevent recurrence.
