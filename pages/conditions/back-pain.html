About This Condition

Understanding Back Pain

Back pain is one of the most commonly mismanaged conditions — often because it is treated generically rather than specifically. At Urmston Physio Clinic, we begin with a thorough assessment to determine the exact source of your pain, whether that is a disc, joint, nerve, muscle, or a combination of factors.

Once we understand the cause, we develop a targeted treatment plan using manual therapy, specific exercise, movement re-education, and lifestyle guidance. Our goal is not just to reduce your pain — it is to address why it occurred in the first place so it does not keep coming back.

Signs & Symptoms

Common Symptoms

These are the most common presentations we see — though every patient is different, and your physiotherapist will assess your individual symptoms thoroughly.

Lower Back Pain

Aching, stiffness, or sharp pain in the lumbar region, often worse in the morning or after prolonged sitting.

Radiating Leg Pain

Pain, tingling, or numbness that travels from the lower back into the buttocks, thigh, or lower leg.

Stiffness and Limited Movement

Difficulty bending, twisting, or straightening up — particularly first thing in the morning.

Muscle Spasm

Involuntary tightening of the back muscles, often triggered by movement and causing sharp pain.

Pain on Prolonged Sitting or Standing

Discomfort that worsens when maintaining the same position for extended periods.

Weakness

Reduced strength in the back, hips, or legs, sometimes accompanied by a feeling of instability.

Our Approach

How We Treat Back Pain

Your treatment plan will be tailored specifically to you — but here is what a typical course of physiotherapy for back pain looks like at Urmston Physio Clinic.

  • 01
    Comprehensive AssessmentA detailed analysis of your movement, posture, muscle strength, and neurological status to identify the exact cause of your back pain.
  • 02
    Manual TherapyHands-on techniques including joint mobilisation, manipulation, and soft tissue treatment to reduce pain and restore movement.
  • 03
    Targeted ExerciseA personalised home exercise programme designed to strengthen the right muscles, improve movement, and support long-term recovery.
  • 04
    Movement Re-educationHelping you understand how you move and correcting patterns that may be contributing to your pain or preventing recovery.
  • 05
    Lifestyle and Activity GuidancePractical advice on how to manage your back in daily life — at work, during exercise, and at rest — to support your recovery and prevent recurrence.
