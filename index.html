Who We Are

Physiotherapy That Goes Further

At Urmston Physio Clinic, we believe the best physiotherapy goes beyond managing symptoms. We identify the true cause of your problem, address it systematically, and equip you with the knowledge to stay well long-term.

Our team of four HCPC-registered physiotherapists brings together decades of specialist expertise across musculoskeletal, neurological, biomechanical, and sports physiotherapy — united by a commitment to exceptional, patient-centred care.
