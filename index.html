Who We Are

Physiotherapy That Goes Further

At Urmston Physio Clinic we go beyond managing symptoms. We find the true cause of your problem, treat it properly, and give you the tools to stay well long term.

Our four HCPC-registered physiotherapists bring together over 95 years of specialist experience across musculoskeletal, neurological, biomechanical, and sports physiotherapy. Whatever brought you to us, you will be seen by someone with the depth of knowledge to help.
