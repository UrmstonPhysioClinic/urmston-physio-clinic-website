Post-Surgery Physio in Urmston

An operation is the start of recovery, not the end. Post-surgery physio helps you get your movement, strength and confidence back at the right pace for your operation, from the first weeks through to work, sport and everyday life.

Who It's For

Physiotherapy After an Operation

Rehab after surgery is what turns a successful operation into getting back to the things you want to do. We help people after planned operations, such as joint replacements, and after surgery following an injury.

Some people come to us as soon as their surgeon or hospital team says they can start physio. Others come later, when progress has stalled or they want more regular sessions.

Our Approach

How We Help

  • 01
    Follow your surgeon's planBring your discharge letter and any rehab guidelines from your surgeon or hospital physio. We work within any restrictions on movement or weight-bearing.
  • 02
    Assess where you areWe check your movement, strength, swelling and walking, and how you're managing at home and at work.
  • 03
    Early recoveryGentle exercises to restore movement and get you walking well, with advice on managing pain and swelling.
  • 04
    Rebuild strengthA progressive programme you can do at home or in the gym. Where it helps, we also use our on-site rehab gym during your appointments.
  • 05
    Back to normalA staged return to work, sport and the activities that matter to you, in line with your surgeon's advice.

When to contact your surgical team or call 999

  • Call 999 for chest pain or sudden breathlessness.
  • Contact your surgical team, GP or NHS 111 the same day if your wound becomes red, hot, swollen or starts to leak, you have a high temperature, you get calf pain with swelling, warmth or redness, or your pain suddenly gets much worse.

Your First Visit

What to Expect

  1. Bring your discharge letter, any rehab guidelines and a list of any restrictions you've been given.
  2. Wear loose clothing so we can see and move the area, for example shorts after hip or knee surgery.
  3. We check how you're moving and agree goals and a plan that fits your surgeon's guidance.
  4. You leave with exercises to start straight away.
