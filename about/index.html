Our Story

About Us

A specialist physiotherapy clinic built on the belief that rigorous assessment, honest communication and genuine patient care produce the best outcomes.

Who We Are

Physiotherapy That Treats the Whole Person

Urmston Physio Clinic is a private musculoskeletal physiotherapy clinic in Urmston, Manchester. We have four HCPC-registered physiotherapists who together bring over 95 years of specialist clinical experience across musculoskeletal, neurological, biomechanical and sports physiotherapy.

Our approach is straightforward: find the actual cause of your problem, treat it properly, and make sure you have what you need to stay well. We do not apply generic treatment to generic diagnoses. Every patient gets a thorough assessment and a plan built specifically for them.

Each member of our team has developed real areas of clinical depth alongside broad MSK expertise. Whatever has brought you to us, you will be seen by someone with the knowledge to help.

The Clinic

Our Facilities

Our large, well-equipped consultation rooms are designed for comprehensive musculoskeletal assessment and treatment, with space to move and be assessed properly. The clinic also has a dedicated rehabilitation gym with a treadmill, exercise bike, weights bench and multigym. Our comfortable reception area and relaxed, welcoming environment make the clinic a place patients feel at ease from the moment they arrive.

Our Principles

How We Work

01

Find the Cause

We never treat symptoms alone. Our assessments look at the whole body, movement patterns, history and lifestyle to understand what is actually driving your problem.

02

Evidence-Informed Practice

Everything we do is grounded in current clinical research. Our team stays up to date and brings the best available evidence to their clinical decisions.

03

Tailored to You

No two patients are the same. Your plan is built around your specific condition, goals, lifestyle and timeline. We work with you, not on you.

04

Long-Term Thinking

We want you to recover properly and stay well. Education and self-management are central to everything we do, not an afterthought.

05

Honest and Transparent

We give you clear, accurate information about your condition and realistic expectations about recovery. You will always know where you stand.

06

Patient Partnership

Recovery works best when patients understand their condition and are active participants. We help you become that patient.
