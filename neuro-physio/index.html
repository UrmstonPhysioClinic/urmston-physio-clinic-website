Neuro Physio in Urmston

Neurological physiotherapy helps people living with conditions that affect the brain, spinal cord and nerves to move, balance and get about as well as they can. Our physios see neuro patients in clinic, and home visits are available with Carl or Roxanna.

Book an Assessment Call 0161 748 4100
Who It's For

Physio for Neurological Conditions

We see people with a range of neurological conditions, including:

  • Stroke
  • Multiple sclerosis (MS)
  • Parkinson's
  • Balance problems and falls
  • Walking and mobility difficulties

Whether your condition is new or long-standing, physio can focus on what matters to you, such as walking further, getting up from a chair more easily or feeling steadier on your feet.

Being Clear

What We Can and Can't Offer

We're an outpatient clinic, not a specialist neuro rehab centre. Here's what that means for you.

What we offer

  • Assessment of your movement, strength, balance and walking
  • Exercise programmes you can keep doing at home
  • Balance work and advice on lowering your risk of falls
  • Hands-on treatment for the stiffness and pain that can come with neurological conditions
  • Home visits with Carl or Roxanna, if getting to the clinic is difficult

What we can't offer

  • Intensive daily rehab or inpatient care
  • Wheelchair, seating or specialist equipment assessments [TO CONFIRM: check this is right]
  • Care from a full neuro team, such as speech and language or occupational therapy

If you need something we can't provide, we'll tell you and point you in the right direction, usually through your GP or your hospital team.

Our Approach

How We Assess and Treat

  • 01
    Getting to know youYour condition, your history, what has changed recently and what you want to work on.
  • 02
    AssessmentMovement, strength, balance and walking, and how you manage everyday tasks.
  • 03
    GoalsWe agree realistic goals with you, based on what matters in your day-to-day life.
  • 04
    TreatmentExercise, balance training, practising everyday tasks, and hands-on treatment where it helps.
  • 05
    Keeping it goingA home programme you can stick to, with family or carers involved if you'd like.

Written by Carl Healy, Clinic Director and MSK Physiotherapist (HCPC registered, PH106684). Last reviewed November 2026.

When to call 999 or see your GP instead

  • Call 999 if you notice signs of a stroke: face drooping, arm weakness or slurred speech, or a sudden severe headache or new double vision.
  • Contact your GP or specialist team if your symptoms change suddenly, for example a sudden worsening of MS symptoms, which could be a relapse.

Meet the team
Your First Visit

What to Expect

  1. We talk through your condition, your history and your goals.
  2. We assess your movement, balance, walking and everyday tasks.
  3. We agree a plan, and treatment usually starts in the same session.
  4. You leave with exercises or advice you can start straight away.

You're welcome to bring a family member or carer. Please bring any letters from your hospital or specialist team, and a list of your medicines.
