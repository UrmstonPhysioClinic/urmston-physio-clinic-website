Who It's For

What Sports Massage Is For

Sports massage is firm, hands-on work on your muscles and soft tissues. People usually book it to:

  • Keep on top of tight muscles during heavy training
  • Get ready for an event, a few days before
  • Recover after a race or a big session
  • Ease general muscle tightness from the gym, work or everyday life

It suits runners, gym-goers and anyone who wants to look after their muscles. It isn't a treatment for injuries or pain that keeps coming back. That's where a physio assessment comes in.

Be Honest With Yourself

Sports Massage or Physio?

We'd rather you book the right thing than the one you came looking for.

Sports massage is a good fit if

  • Your muscles feel tight or tired, but nothing is injured
  • You want regular maintenance during training
  • You're getting ready for, or recovering from, an event

Book a physio assessment instead if

  • You have pain, or an injury you can point to
  • Something keeps coming back, like neck or back pain
  • You're not sure what's wrong

A physio assessment finds the cause of the problem, and your physio can include hands-on soft tissue work as part of your treatment, so you don't miss out on the hands-on side.

Is massage right for you?

  • Don't book a massage if you have a fever or an infection, a suspected blood clot, or an unexplained lump or swelling. See your GP first.
  • Get urgent advice the same day (NHS 111 or your GP) for calf pain with swelling, warmth or redness.
  • If you have a recent injury, a medical condition or you're pregnant, tell us when you book so we can check massage is suitable, or suggest a physio assessment instead.
