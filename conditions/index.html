What We Treat

Conditions We Treat

Our physios treat problems with muscles, joints, tendons and nerves, from everyday aches to sports injuries. Find yours below, or call us on 0161 748 4100 if you're not sure whether we can help.

After an Operation

Rehab After Surgery

An operation is the start of recovery, not the end. A structured rehab programme is what turns a successful operation into getting back to full strength and doing what you want to do. Our physios guide you through it at the right pace, from the first weeks to getting back to work, sport or everyday life.

Hip replacementKnee replacementACL reconstructionRotator cuff repairSpinal surgery
Moving Better

Movement and Biomechanics

Not all physiotherapy is about injury. This is for people who want to move more efficiently, lower their risk of injury or perform better. We look closely at how you move, then work on the patterns that hold you back or leave you prone to problems.
