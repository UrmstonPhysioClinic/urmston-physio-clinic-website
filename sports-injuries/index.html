Sports Injuries

Sports Injury Physiotherapy in Urmston

Whether you've pulled a hamstring at five-a-side, turned an ankle on a trail run, or have a niggle that keeps coming back, we'll find out what's going on and build a plan to get you back to your sport.

What We Treat

Injuries We Often See

Muscle Strains

Hamstring, calf, groin and thigh.

Sprains

Ankle and knee ligaments.

Tendon Problems

Achilles, patellar tendon, and tennis or golfer's elbow.

Running Injuries

Runner's knee, shin pain, plantar fasciitis and pain on the outside of the knee.

Shoulder Pain

From the gym, swimming, climbing or racket sports.

Recurring Niggles

The injury that settles, then comes back every time you build up again.

Our Approach

How We Help

  • 01
    Find the causeA full assessment of what happened, how you move, and what your training looks like.
  • 02
    Settle it downHands-on treatment where it helps, and clear advice on what you can keep doing in the meantime, so you're not stopping more than you need to.
  • 03
    RebuildA progressive strength and movement programme built around your sport, which you can do at home or in your own gym. Where it helps, we also use our on-site rehab gym during your appointments.
  • 04
    Get back to your sportA staged return built around your training, with honest advice about timescales and any match or race dates.
  • 05
    Stay wellWe look at training load, technique and the way you move, to lower the chance of it happening again.
